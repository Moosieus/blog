I keep seeing discussions about Elixir's adoption cropping up. I wanna share my personal thoughts on the subject in one place. The claims I make here are based on my perception of what I see on job boards and in public/prviate discussions.

# The Concern

People are concerned that Elixir's adoption is stagnating. The perception's that the net number of available Elixir jobs isn't growing. While developers with Elixir work experience may find opportunities, newcomers struggle. I don't have some emperical peer-reviewed study to back this, it's just kinda the vibe I'm picking up.

# Developer Adoption

Tech hiring is in a fucked spot right now. It's sufficiently difficult such that many developers, even ones who are especially smart, driven, and capable, must significantly optimize their life around job seeking. This extends to their language choice and preference. Consequently **many developers aren't interested in learning languages they canont find work for.** This applies universally to newer hires and seasoned software engineers seeking change.
